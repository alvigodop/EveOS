WORLD PORTAL — EVE INJECTION PROTOCOL
Protocol version 1
Introduced in World Portal v0.16.0

V0.20 MODEL-NEUTRAL PROTOCOL UPDATE
The canonical plan identity is now world-portal-agent-plan version 1. The legacy
world-portal-eve-plan identity remains accepted for backwards compatibility.
Eve Guided Mode is a user-facing workflow name; the executor and portable Agent
Skill are model-neutral. Context exports may use <world-name>.world-portal-context.zip
and include agent-skill/SKILL.md plus dynamically generated capability and
parameter documentation.


PURPOSE
Eve Guided Mode reduces the operational complexity of Heightmap Forge and the
Orogen Refinement Lab. World Portal exports a bounded description of the active
world. Eve or another compatible adviser may inspect that description and return
a declarative plan. World Portal remains the authority that validates, previews,
executes, saves, and promotes every result.

CANONICAL ROUND TRIP
1. World Portal exports <world-name>.eve-context.zip.
2. The user uploads the ZIP to Eve or another compatible analysis client.
3. The adviser returns <world-name>.eve-plan.json.
4. World Portal validates the plan without executing it.
5. The user reviews the objective, selected inputs, warnings, and confirmations.
6. World Portal invokes only installed, allow-listed capabilities.
7. Derived outputs remain attached to the active world and preserve lineage.
8. Canonical promotion requires explicit user confirmation.
9. World Portal can export the best current Orogen input bundle.

AUTHORITY BOUNDARY
Eve is an adviser and orchestrator. Eve may:
- inspect exported metadata, previews, statistics, sessions, and passes;
- choose among operations World Portal explicitly advertises;
- recommend settings and selected layers;
- return a deterministic action plan.

Eve does not own world data and does not execute arbitrary code. World Portal:
- owns every world and layer;
- verifies world and context identity;
- validates parameter ranges and layer compatibility;
- performs all image processing locally;
- controls canonical promotion;
- stores execution history and resulting assets.

CONTEXT BUNDLE
The context ZIP contains compact, machine-readable and human-readable context:
- manifest.json: protocol, world identity, context hash, and export policy;
- world-summary.json: active world, canonical layers, and ownership counts;
- capabilities.json: commands supported by the installed World Portal version;
- layers.json: layer roles, provenance, dimensions, hashes, status, and analysis;
- sessions.json: grouped Orogen analysis sessions;
- passes.json: non-destructive refinement lineage;
- statistics.json: physical and raster intelligence per layer;
- current-settings.json: active World Portal and tool settings;
- chat-report.txt: concise world summary for conversation;
- layer-reports/: detailed plain-language intelligence reports;
- previews/: compact PNG previews for relevant layers;
- selected-assets/: optional full-resolution canonical visual, mask, and heightmap;
- asset-index.json: IDs mapped to preview and report paths.

DEEP RASTER INTELLIGENCE
World Portal exposes deterministic evidence rather than only image filenames.

Mask intelligence may include:
- raw and spherical area-corrected land coverage;
- connected landmasses and tiny-component counts;
- largest and top-three landmass shares;
- effective landmass count and fragmentation;
- component size distribution and patch density;
- geographic centroid and latitude/hemisphere shares;
- occupied longitude and latitude extent;
- antimeridian, seam, and pole contact;
- coastline edges, edge density, and perimeter-area complexity;
- coastal-pixel share and land at least 2, 4, 8, or 16 pixels inland;
- largest component centroids, shares, bounds, and compactness;
- anomaly flags for global land, near-empty masks, fragmentation, and speckles.

Heightmap intelligence may include:
- land coverage and spherical coverage;
- minimum, maximum, mean, relief, and standard deviation;
- P05 through P99 elevation percentiles;
- P90-P10 terrain contrast;
- hypsometric integral and elevation entropy;
- lowland, hill, mountain, and peak shares;
- local roughness and slope proxies;
- ridge, valley, and flat-neighborhood shares;
- peak candidates, highland systems, and highest-point coordinates;
- near-black land risk and clipped-peak risk;
- latitude-band elevation means and elevation center;
- anomaly flags for global elevation, low relief, clipping, and fragile land.

Visual raster intelligence may include:
- dimensions, megapixels, aspect ratio, and exact 2:1 status;
- alpha/transparency coverage;
- exact-black, exact-white, near-black, and near-white shares;
- luminance mean, variation, percentiles, and dynamic range;
- RGB channel means and variation;
- grayscale share, saturation, and quantized color count;
- color entropy, palette concentration, and dominant colors;
- texture complexity and edge strength;
- conservative role hints such as likely mask or likely heightmap.

Pair intelligence may include:
- mask IoU, Dice, pixel agreement, and spherical agreement;
- source-only and comparison-only land;
- shared heightmap land, elevation error, bias, and correlation;
- visual luminance/RGB difference and materially changed pixel share.

These measurements do not create lore, political boundaries, biome names, or
palette meanings. Unknown classified colors remain unknown until a legend or
user mapping is supplied.

PLAN DOCUMENT
An Agent plan is JSON with this identity:
- protocol: world-portal-agent-plan (canonical) or world-portal-eve-plan (legacy alias)
- version: 1
- worldId: the target world
- basedOnContextHash: the exported context hash
- title and plain-language summary
- riskLevel
- commands
- expectedOutputs
- notes

A command contains:
- id;
- capability;
- input layer references;
- parameters;
- reason;
- expected result;
- confirmation requirement;
- failure behavior.

Plans may not contain JavaScript, HTML, shell commands, executable attachments,
filesystem paths, action-target URLs, or unknown command types.

VERSION 1 CAPABILITIES
The installed capability manifest is the source of truth. v0.16.0 advertises:
- heightmapForge.setParameters
- heightmapForge.regenerateMask
- heightmapForge.regenerateElevation
- refinement.selectLayers
- refinement.compareMasks
- layers.markProvisional
- layers.promoteCanonical
- orogen.exportInputBundle
- world.saveCheckpoint

Future commands are not executable until a later World Portal version advertises
them. A plan generated for a newer capability set must be rejected safely.

VALIDATION AND DRY RUN
Before execution World Portal checks:
- protocol and version;
- world ID;
- context hash and stale-state risk;
- referenced layer existence;
- installed capability support;
- unsafe or executable-like values;
- canonical confirmation requirements.

A context-hash mismatch is a warning because the active world may have changed
since export. The user should export a new context or apply only compatible steps.

NON-DESTRUCTIVE LINEAGE
Eve-directed operations create derived layers, provisional states, checkpoints,
and refinement history. Original source maps are never silently overwritten.
Canonical promotion remains a user decision and preserves parent IDs.

OFFLINE-FIRST RULE
The ZIP/JSON round trip is the canonical protocol. A future live MCP, local
agent, Eve OS bridge, or desktop connector must call the same capabilities and
schemas rather than creating a second unrestricted automation system.

v0.16.1 COMMAND-RESULT AND FINALIZATION CONTRACT
=================================================
Heightmap Forge regeneration commands must persist full-resolution output layers. Their results expose generatedLayerId plus domain-specific mask or heightmap IDs.

A later command may reference an earlier result with:
  $result.<commandId>.<field>
or:
  { "fromCommand": "<commandId>", "field": "<field>" }

References may target only commands that appear earlier in the plan. World Portal resolves them locally during execution.

orogen.finalizeInput and orogen.exportInputBundle accept optional maskLayerId and heightmapLayerId inputs plus outputWidth, outputHeight, coastFloor, maskThreshold, strictBinaryMask, and requireMatchingLandSupport parameters.

The finalizer is authoritative for downloadable Orogen inputs. It must produce a strict binary mask, exact-black ocean, nonzero accepted land, and identical mask/heightmap support. It must re-decode encoded PNGs before declaring the result Orogen Ready. Checksums in the manifest refer to the exact downloadable PNG bytes.

When newer generated outputs exist but are not explicitly selected or promoted, implicit canonical export is forbidden. World Portal must pause and request a final layer choice.

v0.19 MISSION ORCHESTRATION CONTRACT
====================================
Plans may create or recover missions, attach baselines, resolve semantic layer
selectors, repair prerequisites with confirmation, generate candidate styles,
pause for review, and resume from persisted command results. Candidate
selection is separate from canonical promotion. Context bundles expose mission
control, missing prerequisites, selector roles, candidate state, pending
decisions, capabilities, and parameter ranges even when no mission exists.
