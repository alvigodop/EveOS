WORLD PORTAL — OROGEN EVIDENCE ASSIMILATION DOCTRINE
=====================================================

PURPOSE
World Orogen runs may be physically invalid in one role while still containing
valuable evidence in another. World Portal must judge each imported layer by its
role instead of accepting or rejecting an entire run as one indivisible result.

HARD SKELETON AND SOFT EVIDENCE
The current canonical visual map, canonical coastline mask, and canonical
heightmap are the world's hard mechanical skeleton. Imported Orogen masks,
heightmaps, satellite maps, climate maps, terrain maps, and classified rasters are
soft evidence until explicitly promoted.

A globally contaminated mask must never define coastline truth. A globally
contaminated heightmap may still contribute interior ridges after it is clipped to
a trusted mask. A satellite layer may be useful for land coloration while its
ocean is unusable. A climate raster may provide measured palette and latitude-band
evidence without establishing named biomes.

ROLE-SPECIFIC TRUST
Every evidence layer may store independent trust values for:
- coastline;
- height and interior relief;
- visual land appearance;
- climate or environmental classification.

Statuses are canonical-safe, provisional, anomalous-but-useful, rejected, and
archived. Automatic anomaly penalties are advisory. User edits and Eve plans must
remain explicit, versioned, and reproducible.

FINALIZATION STYLES
Clean / Canonical:
- retains the accepted coastline;
- removes detached noise;
- minimizes evidence-driven terrain changes;
- prioritizes simulator safety and repeatability.

Hybrid / Balanced:
- rejects remote specks;
- retains supported coastline bends and protrusions;
- preserves nearby companion islands above an area threshold;
- recovers useful ridge and valley detail inside the accepted land support.

Feature-Preserving:
- allows a wider evidence buffer around the canonical coast;
- accepts lower multi-source support when evidence trust is sufficient;
- preserves more nearby islands and local coastline character;
- retains stronger interior ridge and relief variation.

Cleanest does not automatically mean best. Each style creates a child layer and a
reversible refinement pass. No style silently replaces canonical geography.

COASTLINE CHARACTER RULE
The system must distinguish bad noise from valuable irregularity. Bad noise
includes remote specks, ocean interpreted as land, encoding artifacts, and
unsupported isolated components. Valuable irregularity may include nearby island
groups, distinctive peninsulas, bays, concave or convex bends, and unusual but
supported protrusions.

Character recovery uses weighted evidence support, connected-component area, and
distance from the canonical coastline. Horizontal equirectangular edges wrap.
Remote components remain rejected even in Feature-Preserving mode unless the user
changes the thresholds deliberately.

HEIGHT EVIDENCE RULE
Orogen height evidence is normalized only inside the selected land mask. The
source canonical heightmap supplies the stable baseline. Evidence may contribute
broad elevation and high-frequency interior detail. Ridge and valley retention,
contrast, and smoothing are independently tunable.

Every generated heightmap must keep ocean at exact zero and accepted land at one
through 255. It must not create land outside the paired mask.

VISUAL EVIDENCE RULE
Land-only synthesis keeps the canonical visual map's ocean unchanged and blends a
trusted satellite or terrain layer only inside the chosen mask. The result is a
new visual child layer. The original visual map remains available.

CLIMATE AND ENVIRONMENTAL ZONES
Climate, biome, and classified rasters may be measured within the canonical land
mask. World Portal may store dominant colors, relative coverage, and latitude-band
patterns. It may create provisional color-derived environmental-zone layers.

Without a supplied Orogen legend, those zones must not be named as deserts,
forests, tundra, Köppen classes, or story regions. They are physical evidence, not
lore or canon.

EVE CONTEXT CURATION
Archive broadly and analyze selectively. Eve context should prioritize canonical
layers, the newest mission run, one useful prior comparison, and distinct
anomalous evidence. Duplicate historical rasters may remain stored locally without
being included full-resolution in every context bundle.

EVE capabilities may set trust, build character-preserving masks, assimilate
heightmaps, clip contaminated heightmaps, extract climate metadata, build
provisional environmental zones, and create a paired next-pass refinement. All
commands remain declarative and allow-listed.

UPSTREAM SEPARATION
Orogen remains a separately maintained GPLv3 upstream module. Evidence
assimilation belongs to World Portal. It consumes imported rasters and typed-array
results through adapters and does not modify or absorb Orogen source code.
