WORLD PORTAL REFINEMENT MISSION DOCTRINE
Version 1

PURPOSE
Refinement Mission Mode is a state-driven operator over existing World Portal
engines. It does not implement a second terrain algorithm. Heightmap Forge,
Orogen Refinement Lab, Eve Guided Mode, and the Orogen finalizer remain the
authoritative transformation systems.

MISSION OWNERSHIP
A mission belongs to one world. It owns references to that world's canonical
baseline, exported Orogen inputs, returned Orogen sessions, comparisons, Eve
context and plans, accepted outputs, checkpoints, and pass lineage. Deleting the
world deletes the mission with all other owned metadata.

ONE NEXT ACTION
The mission must expose one primary action based on persistent state. Advanced
controls remain available underneath, but ordinary refinement should not require
the user to manually remember filenames, layer IDs, canonical selections, or the
last completed step.

AUTOMATIC INTAKE
Orogen files may be grouped by pass token and inferred role. Confidence and
dimension requirements are controlled by the mission accuracy profile. Low-
confidence files are not silently attached under strict profiles. Missing or
anomalous files remain provisional evidence and are never promoted automatically.

ACCURACY PROFILES
Fast prioritizes speed and small context. Balanced is the default. High Accuracy
uses larger previews, stricter matching, and recommends full-resolution evidence.
Forensic includes all relevant evidence and requires very high role confidence.
Profiles tune evidence scope and automatic decisions, not the canonical world
without confirmation.

EVE CURATION
Mission-aware Eve context must include the baseline, latest returned Orogen run,
comparison results, anomalies, lineage, tool settings, installed capabilities,
and current next action. Full-resolution assets remain opt-in or profile-
recommended to control bundle size.

CHECKPOINTS AND ROLLBACK
Accepted mission steps create checkpoints containing canonical visual, mask, and
heightmap references. Rollback restores references without deleting newer layers,
sessions, or evidence. Original source maps remain non-destructive.

OROGEN SEPARATION
World Orogen remains an external, separately maintained GPLv3 module. Mission
Mode exchanges finalized inputs and imported outputs through World Portal data
boundaries. It does not modify or absorb Orogen source code.

v0.19 EVE-CONTROLLED MISSION LIFECYCLE
======================================
Refinement Missions may be created and managed through allow-listed Eve plan
commands. Mission scope is explicitly world-agnostic and may target a planet,
continent, island, region, or custom map. Candidate generation creates Clean,
Hybrid, and Feature-Preserving pairs for human review. A paused plan persists
its command position and results and may resume after the user selects a
candidate. Selection does not silently promote canonical data.
