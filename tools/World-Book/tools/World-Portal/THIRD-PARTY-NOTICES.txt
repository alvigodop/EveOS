HEX RENDERING NOTE
No H3 library is bundled. Research into H3 informed the distinction between a
true discrete global grid and this project's all-hex visual texture mosaic.


v0.8.1 IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
- Three.js OrbitControls is used with projection-specific enableRotate,
  enablePan, mouse button, and touch mappings.
- The procedural cloud image is generated at runtime with Three.js
  CanvasTexture.
- The NASA Blue Marble map remains the geographic source.


v0.10.0 COUNTRY GEOGRAPHY SOURCES
- Natural Earth project categories informed the country, rivers, lakes, and
  coastline layer design. Natural Earth data is free for use.
- Country outline files are Natural Earth-derived geometry bundled through the
  installed CountryInfo dataset.
- River lines and GSHHS lake polygons are read from Basemap's bundled map data.
- WGS84 geodesic area and length estimates are generated with pyproj.Geod.
- These layers are generalized and are not suitable for legal boundaries,
  cadastral measurement, navigation, or a complete hydrology census.

fetches the pinned upstream source into the submodule's own repository and
populates outer/orogen. A distribution that includes those fetched files must
ship Orogen's LICENSE and satisfy all applicable GPLv3 obligations.

RELATIONSHIP AND COUPLING: The architecture is designed for separately
maintained and separately licensed programs. World Portal does not import
Orogen's modules, merge import maps, modify its source, control its settings UI,
or use its DOM as a general automation API. World Portal-owned,
versioned/world-keyed connectors provide finalized file/data handoff and intake,
guarded startup-heightmap sync, source-commit provenance, returned-mask
extractors, a manual read-only update notice, and a camera-interactive connected
mirror of the rendered planet/galaxy view.

planet-mirror@1 remains rendered visual transport. For the pinned Orogen commit,
World Portal also owns a same-origin, exact-context, commit-audited compatibility
relay to the primary render canvas. It forwards only primary-button drag when
unmodified, wheel-family zoom, and host pinch translated to wheel, for camera
orbit and zoom. Ctrl+wheel is allowed only because Chromium emits trackpad pinch
that way; Alt/Meta/Shift-modified wheel is rejected, and no keyboard event is
forwarded. It never forwards settings UI, right/middle buttons, edit gestures,
or data actions. A semantic planet-camera-input@1 postMessage
capability is preferred for a future upstream revision; the pinned commit does
not implement it.

Heightmap Forge was implemented independently as a World Portal converter and
produces Orogen-compatible input through its own modules.
